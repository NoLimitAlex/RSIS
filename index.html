01 / What

A student-led investment society at Ressu upper secondary school in Helsinki, inspired by the investment societies of universities such as Bocconi and LSE.

02 / Goal

To give students the resources, the knowledge and early, relevant experience in quantitative finance, corporate finance and M&A, equity research and financial markets.
